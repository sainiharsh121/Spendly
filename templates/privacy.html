Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

01Data We Collect

We collect the information you provide when you create an account, such as your name and email address, and the expense details you log, including amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions.

02How We Use Your Data

Your data is used only to operate Spendly: to show your expenses, produce summaries and category breakdowns, and keep your account secure. We do not sell your personal information or use it for advertising.

03Data Storage

Your data is stored securely and retained for as long as your account is active. You can request deletion of your account and all associated expense data at any time, after which it will be removed from our systems.

04Third Party Services

Spendly loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address when pages load. We do not share your expense data with any third party, except where required by law.

05Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or want to request access to or deletion of your data, please get in touch with the Spendly team.
